# NimbusCRM Pricing

## Plans overview
NimbusCRM has three plans. All prices are per user, per month, billed monthly. Annual billing gives a 20% discount.

## Starter plan
$12 per user per month. For small teams of up to 5 users. Includes lead management, follow-up reminders by email, the pipeline board, basic reports and the mobile app.

## Pro plan
$29 per user per month. For growing sales teams with no user limit. Includes everything in Starter plus WhatsApp reminders, Gmail and Outlook integration, conversion reports and forecasting.

## Business plan
$49 per user per month. Includes everything in Pro plus custom roles and permissions, audit logs, single sign-on (SSO) and a dedicated account manager.

## Discounts
Annual billing gives 20% off any plan. Registered non-profits get an extra 10% off. Any other discount must be approved by the sales team.
