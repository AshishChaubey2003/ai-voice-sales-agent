# NimbusCRM Features

## Lead and contact management
NimbusCRM stores leads, contacts and companies in one place. Each lead has an owner, a stage (New, Contacted, Qualified, Proposal, Won, Lost) and a full activity timeline. Leads can be imported from CSV files.

## Follow-up reminders
Sales reps can set follow-up reminders on any lead. Reminders appear in the NimbusCRM dashboard and are sent by email. The Pro and Business plans also send reminders on WhatsApp. If a lead has no activity for 7 days, NimbusCRM flags it as going cold.

## Sales pipeline
Deals move through a drag-and-drop pipeline board. Managers can see pipeline value by stage and by sales rep.

## Email integration
NimbusCRM connects to Gmail and Outlook. Emails with a lead are logged on the lead's timeline automatically.

## Reports
The Starter plan includes basic activity reports. Pro and Business plans add conversion reports and a monthly forecast.

## Mobile app
Android and iOS apps are available on all plans. Reps can log calls and update deals from their phone.

## What NimbusCRM does not include
NimbusCRM does not include a built-in phone dialer, marketing email campaigns or invoicing. It does not offer on-premise installation; it is cloud-only.
