# NimbusCRM FAQ

## Free trial
NimbusCRM offers a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card is needed to start the trial.

## Onboarding and setup
Most teams are set up in one day. Pro and Business customers get a free 60-minute onboarding call. Data can be imported from CSV files or from HubSpot and Zoho CRM exports.

## Support
Starter customers get email support, answered within one business day. Pro customers get email and chat support. Business customers get priority support and a dedicated account manager.

## Data security
Data is stored on servers in India and encrypted in transit and at rest. Customers can export all their data at any time.

## Cancellation
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stop at the end of the billing month. Annual plans are refunded only for unused full months within the first 30 days.

## Talking to sales
Visitors can ask to be contacted by the sales team. The sales team works Monday to Friday, 10 am to 7 pm India time.
